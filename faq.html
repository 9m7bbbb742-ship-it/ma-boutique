Questions, answered.

Quick answers to the things people ask most. Still stuck? Contact us.

How much does shipping cost?

Shipping is a flat fee, and it becomes free once your order reaches the free-shipping amount shown in your cart. See the Shipping page for details.

Can I return an item?

Yes. Read our returns policy on the Returns page for the conditions and the steps to follow.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Payments are processed securely by Stripe. You can pay by credit or debit card and by the digital wallets offered at checkout. We never see or store your card details.

How can I check on my order?

Send us a message with your name and the email used at checkout, and we will tell you where your order stands.

Do you ship outside Canada?

Our checkout shows the countries we currently ship to. If yours is missing, contact us.

How do I contact you?

Use the form on the Contact page. We read every message.
