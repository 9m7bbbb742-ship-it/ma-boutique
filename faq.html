Questions, answered.

Everything people ask us most, in plain language. If your question is not here, our contact page and our assistant are always ready to help.

How much does shipping cost?

Shipping is a simple flat fee, shown in your cart before you pay. Your final shipping and any taxes are confirmed at checkout, so there are no surprises. The Shipping page has the full details.

Is there free shipping?

Yes. Once your order reaches the free-shipping amount, delivery is on us. Your cart shows a progress bar so you always know how close you are.

How do I follow my order?

Open the Track order page and enter your order number and the email you used at checkout. You will see your order move from paid to shipped to delivered, along with a tracking number as soon as one is available.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Add your items to the cart, check out and you are done. Your order number and email are all you need to follow your order later.

Is it safe to pay on your site?

Yes. Payments are processed by Stripe, one of the most widely used payment platforms in the world. Your card details are entered on Stripe's secure page and never touch our site or our database.

Which payment methods can I use?

You can pay by credit or debit card, and by the digital wallets Stripe offers on your device when they are available. The options you see at checkout are the ones you can use.

Can I return an item?

Our returns policy explains exactly what can be returned, how long you have and what to do next. Read it on the Returns page before you buy, so you always know where you stand.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Contact us as soon as possible with your order number. If your order has not shipped yet, we will do our best to help.

Which countries do you ship to?

The countries we currently ship to are listed during checkout. If yours is missing, let us know on the contact page and we will keep it in mind.

What if a product is sold out?

Sold-out products are clearly marked. Check back soon, or write to us and tell us what you are looking for.

How do I reach a real person?

Use the form on the Contact page. We read every message and reply by email. For quick questions about products, shipping or returns, you can also ask our assistant.

Still curious?

Send us your question and we will get back to you by email.
